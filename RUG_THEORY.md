# Rug Theory

**SATIRE — fictional laws, fictional disciplinary terminology, no physical predictions.**

## Abstract

We introduce the rug as a candidate mediator between the left-hand library and
the right-hand recording studio. Its principal observable is that people keep
mentioning it. After an exhaustive investigation consisting of looking at the
picture again, we conclude that further sitting is required.

## The Rug Principle

A sufficiently well-positioned rug can cause a room to be described as
“together,” even when its occupants are pursuing several incompatible deadlines.

This principle has not been quantified. The measuring tape is under a manuscript.

## Five laws of Dude Physics

1. **Conservation of unfinished projects.** Moving a manuscript from the desk to
   the chair changes its location without reducing the amount of work remaining.
2. **Spontaneous symmetry lounging.** An empty chair permits many possible
   sitting orientations. Occupancy chooses one and calls the others theoretical.
3. **The uncertainty of the patch cable.** The better you know which socket a
   cable leaves, the more likely its destination is behind the synthesizer.
4. **The monolith boundary condition.** A black rectangular object increases the
   apparent seriousness of everything within three feet. It does not repair a proof.
5. **The rug's right of refusal.** A carpet may connect a room aesthetically while
   declining responsibility for its owner's theory of the universe.
